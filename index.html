What is inside

Meals

Recipes with calorie estimates, a plan for the week, and ideas from a photo of your fridge. Your own recipes are welcome: typed, spoken, or snapped as a picture. Calorie numbers are estimates, shown as about.

Gym

Clear moving pictures show each exercise, with a built in timer and rep counting, so you never leave the app. Snap a photo of a machine and get a routine for it.

Calendar

Habits and to dos in your own messy words, or spoken out loud. The app sorts them into days. Undone things simply wait. Nothing shames you.

Bad day plan

Say it is a Bad day and the plan goes softer: lighter sessions, gentler meals, room for a treat. Great days can stretch a little further, only if you want.

Questions

Is Fit Hard free?

Yes. Your first month is the full app, free, with no card. After that, a free plan keeps the everyday tools, and Pro is there if you want more.

Do I need a gym?

No. You tell the app what you have, at home or at a gym, and your routines are built from that.

What happens on a bad day?

You check in as Bad, Ok or Great each morning. On a Bad day the plan goes softer: lighter sessions and more room. Nothing is taken away to punish you.

Does it judge treats?

Never. Treats are part of life. Fit Hard keeps them outside your meal plan and never blames you for them.

Is Fit Hard medical advice?

No. Fit Hard is a tracker and a planner. It is not a doctor and gives no medical advice. Calorie numbers are estimates, not exact counts.
